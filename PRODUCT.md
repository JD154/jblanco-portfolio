## Platform

web

## Users

The primary audiences are, in equal measure:

- Recruiters and hiring managers evaluating Jesus Blanco for senior front-end roles.
- Companies, founders, and product teams evaluating Jesus Blanco for project work.

Visitors need to understand his capabilities and experience, review representative work, and decide whether to contact him.

## Product Purpose

This portfolio presents Jesus Blanco's professional profile, selected front-end work, and contact channels. It should make his experience and strengths easy to evaluate and convert relevant employment or project interest into direct contact.

Success means a qualified visitor can assess fit through the CV and project evidence, then reach Jesus through email, LinkedIn, or GitHub.

## Positioning

Jesus Blanco is a senior front-end developer who combines broad product-facing frontend execution with particular strength in clean, maintainable, and scalable interfaces, reusable UI systems, and complex data visualization.

His work spans application interfaces, design systems and component libraries, investor workflows, and data-rich dashboards across React, Vue, TypeScript, and related web technologies.

## Operating Context

The portfolio is a public, bilingual English and Spanish website. Visitors can:

- Review a concise professional introduction and personal working philosophy.
- Download the current CV.
- inspect selected projects, their descriptions, technology stacks, screenshots, and available demos.
- Switch between English and Spanish and between light and dark themes.
- Contact Jesus through email, LinkedIn, or GitHub.
